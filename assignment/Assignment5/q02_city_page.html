Discover Mathura

History Culture Food Visit

History

Mathura is one of the oldest cities in India and sits on the banks of the Yamuna river.

Culture

Festivals such as Holi and Janmashtami fill the city with colour, music and devotion.

Food

Famous for its peda, kachori and lassi, the local food is loved by visitors.

Visit

The best time to visit is from October to March, when the weather is pleasant.
