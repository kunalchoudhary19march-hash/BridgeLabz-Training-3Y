The Art of Typography

A short article on text styling

Why Fonts Matter

Typography is the craft of making written language clear, readable and visually appealing. A good choice of fonts can shape how a reader feels about a page before they read a single word.

Designers often pair a serif font for headings with a sans-serif font for body text. Font weight adds hierarchy, while size guides the reader's eyes from the most important content to the least.

Monospace fonts, such as Courier New, are common for code and technical writing.

Finally, spacing matters as much as the letters themselves. Comfortable line-height and gentle letter-spacing make long paragraphs easier to read.
